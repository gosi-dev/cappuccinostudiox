Questions

Before you book

How does payment work?
Creative Strategist is paid at the start of each month. The Edit Sprint and Content Engine are 50% to book, 50% when the final files land. Invoices are in EUR, paid by bank transfer.
How fast can you start?
Kick-off within 2 working days of the deposit. The Edit Sprint delivers 3 days later, the Content Engine 7 days later.
How many rounds of changes do I get?
Two per video on the Edit Sprint and the Content Engine. On Creative Strategist, changes happen inside the month. Extra rounds are quoted before I start them.
Do you post on my account?
Only on Creative Strategist, which includes scheduling, comments and replies. The other two hand you finished files to post yourself — the Content Engine adds a 30-day calendar, the Edit Sprint is just the cut files.
Who owns the work?
You do, once the final invoice is paid. I keep the right to show the work in the portfolio unless you ask me not to.
