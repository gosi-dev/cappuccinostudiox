A world built around one love song

Inkreece is an Afrobeats artist with more than one side. We built a launch that shows all of him at once.

For the single “Onyeoma” we made a fictional romance in a 3D, anime-led world. It gave the song a story people could follow, and gave the artist a world he can keep selling into.
